What is Solar Energy?

Turning sunlight into useful power.

Solar energy is energy from the Sun. Solar panels use photovoltaic cells to convert sunlight into electrical energy that can be used in homes, schools, offices and other places.

A solar system can generate electricity during daylight hours and can work with batteries or the electricity grid to support energy needs.

How It Works

From sunlight to electricity.

The basic solar power process can be understood in three simple steps.

☀

1. Sunlight

Sunlight reaches the photovoltaic cells inside solar panels and provides the energy needed to start the process.

ϟ

2. Electricity

Photovoltaic cells convert sunlight into direct-current electrical energy.

⌁

3. Usable Power

An inverter converts the electricity into a form that can power everyday electrical devices.

Benefits

Why solar energy matters.

Solar power offers several practical and environmental benefits when used appropriately.

Renewable Source

Sunlight is a naturally replenishing source of energy available every day.

Lower Emissions

Generating electricity from sunlight can reduce reliance on fossil-fuel-based electricity.

Energy Independence

Solar systems can help homes and organisations generate part of their own electricity.

Long-Term Value

Once installed, solar panels can continue producing electricity for many years with proper care.
