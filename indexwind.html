How It Works

Turning moving air into electricity.

Wind turbines use the movement of air to rotate large blades. This rotation drives a generator, which produces electricity that can be supplied to homes, businesses and communities.

01

Wind Moves the Blades

Moving air pushes the turbine blades and makes them rotate.

02

Rotor Spins the Generator

The rotating blades transfer mechanical energy toward the generator.

03

Electricity Is Produced

The generator converts mechanical energy into electrical energy.

Wind Energy

Understanding wind power

Wind power depends on several factors, including wind speed, turbine design, location and available land. These factors influence how much electricity a turbine can produce.

01

Wind Speed

Stronger and more consistent winds can provide better conditions for electricity generation.

02

Turbine Size

Turbine height, blade length and generator capacity affect energy production.

03

Location

Wind farms are generally planned in locations with suitable and reliable wind conditions.

The Future of Wind

Cleaner energy, smarter technology.

Better Turbine Design

Offshore Wind

Offshore projects use wind resources available over seas and oceans.
